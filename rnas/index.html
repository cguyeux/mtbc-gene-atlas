Stable and regulatory RNAs

The 142 RNA entities annotated in H37Rv (Mycobrowser catalogue): small regulatory RNAs, transfer RNAs and ribosomal RNAs. They are served as a separate track: they are not coding genes, they are counted apart from the gene set and the still-unknown stock, and they receive none of the protein layers (Pfam, structure prediction, orthology, proteomics), which do not apply to RNA.

What this atlas adds to the legacy record is its own population signal: intra-MTBC variation over 145,209 genomes, with an explicit coverage control — a region whose neighbourhood is itself variant-poor cannot be called constrained. No function is inferred here; the product shown is Mycobrowser's.
