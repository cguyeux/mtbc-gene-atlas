# Data licence

The annotation records and derived data in this repository (the gene catalogue
`site/content/gene_xref.tsv` and the per-gene records `site/content/genes/*.json`)
are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(CC-BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

You are free to share and adapt this material for any purpose, provided you give
appropriate credit (see Citation in `README.md`).

## Third-party sources

Individual fiches integrate and redistribute annotations from external databases,
which retain the licences of their respective sources. Notably UniProt and STRING are
distributed under CC-BY 4.0; Pfam, eggNOG and the other databases cited on each fiche
carry their own terms. Each per-gene record lists, in its Sources section, the
provenance of every field so that the origin and licence of any redistributed
annotation can be traced.

The pipeline and application code are covered separately by the MIT licence (see
`LICENSE`).
