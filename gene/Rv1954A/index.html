Annotation: from legacy to revised

Legacy (H37Rv / Mycobrowser)Hypothetical protein
MTBC0 PGAP re-annotation—
Revised (this work)Secreted immunomodulatory protein with intrinsically disordered regions and B/T-cell epitopes. Treatment of macrophages with recombinant Rv1954A triggers TLR4-mediated activation with increased IL-12 and TNF-alpha secretion, and upregulates CD80/CD86/CD40/MHC I-II, consistent with a role in enhancing antigen presentation. A Rv1954A knock-in M. smegmatis strain (Ms_Rv1954A) shows increased macrophage infectivity, induces ROS/RNS, and Rv1954A itself protects against oxidative/nitrosative stress, enhancing intracellular survival. Mice immunized with Ms_Rv1954A mount a Th1 response; intratracheal infection causes lymphocyte infiltration without granuloma formation. Rv1954A is immunogenic (elevated IgG titres in PTB patients vs controls), suggesting utility as a diagnostic marker (Arora et al. 2020, Front Cell Infect Microbiol, DOI 10.3389/fcimb.2020.564565). Single-study origin, not yet independently replicated -- confidence kept at medium rather than high. No Sec/Tat signal peptide and no transmembrane helix are predicted here (DeepTMHMM, confirmed by this atlas' Kyte-Doolittle second opinion); the same layer also calls the canonical ESX substrates ESAT-6 (Rv3875) and CFP-10 (Rv3874) globular, so if this protein is exported it is by a route that leaves no recognisable signal.

In the literature (TB corpus sweep) characterised phenotype

1 TB publication mentions this gene. A dedicated study (Sharma et al., Front Cell Infect Microbiol 2020) reports Rv1954A as a SECRETED protein exclusive to pathogenic mycobacteria (present but not secreted in BCG) that acts as a **TLR4 agonist**: cytokine induction (IL-12, TNF-alpha) in macrophages is abolished specifically in a TLR4-knockout background but not TLR2-knockout, and a M. smegmatis knock-in expressing Rv1954A shows enhanced phagocytosis, prolonged intracellular survival under oxidative/nitrosative stress, increased apoptosis induction, upregulated macrophage maturation markers (CD80/CD86/CD40/MHC-I/II), a Th1-biased response in mice, and TB-patient-specific serum IgG reactivity (proposed as a diagnostic candidate). No independent replication found in a dedicated literature search (2026-08-11). A receptor-knockout result is stronger evidence than a fold-homology claim, but remains a single, non-replicated study: treat as a strong lead for formal requalification, not yet a settled function. reformulation, not a quotation

Conservation & selection (intra-MTBC, 145 209 strains)

pN/pS 0.697 · relaxed/neutral
Polymorphic sites (≥ 0.1% of strains) 1 synonymous, 2 missense, 0 nonsense, 0 frameshift

pN/pS from segregating SNPs (singletons removed) normalised by possible sites. Low pN/pS = purifying selection (a strong signal that a "hypothetical" is a real, constrained gene). A high pN/pS is ambiguous: relaxed constraint or positive selection (drug resistance, antigenic variation) inflate it; e.g. rpoB/katG/pncA score high here for resistance, not loss of function. A clonal disruption (one allele over a clade) suggests lineage pseudogenisation; a convergent one (many independent alleles) is typical of resistance loss-of-function.

Detection by mass spectrometry is direct, experimental evidence that the protein product exists — orthogonal to sequence conservation and to Tn-seq essentiality, and especially decisive for a "hypothetical" locus. Read the dataset count as abundance, not as replication. Measured across this atlas (3,491 detected proteins), the number of datasets a protein appears in tracks its abundance almost monotonically: median 1.2 ppm at 1 dataset of 16, 27 ppm at 10, 392 ppm at 15, 1,325 ppm at 16 — three orders of magnitude. A high count therefore says the protein is abundant more than it says several independent experiments confirmed it. What the count does establish, at any value, is that the product exists.

A verified absence of signals, which is not a compartment. No Sec/Tat signal peptide, no transmembrane helix and no lipobox were found on this sequence. The predictor reports topology and export signals; it does not measure where the protein ends up, so this page draws no cytoplasmic conclusion from it. In M. tuberculosis that reservation is not theoretical: the 12 Esx paralogues of this atlas — EsxA/ESAT-6 and EsxB/CFP-10 among them, the best-established secreted proteins of the species — carry this very call, because substrates of the type VII (ESX) secretion system leave no recognisable signal. « Secreted » and « no signal peptide » are true together here.

Evidence

  • Recombinant Rv1954A activates macrophages via TLR4, inducing IL-12/TNF-alpha secretion and CD80/CD86/CD40/MHC I-II upregulation (Arora et al. 2020, PMID 33163415).
  • Rv1954A knock-in M. smegmatis (Ms_Rv1954A) shows increased macrophage infectivity and survival, with protection against oxidative/nitrosative stress conferred by Rv1954A itself (same source).
  • Immunogenic in vivo: Th1 response in immunized mice, elevated IgG titres in PTB patients vs controls (same source).
  • Independently referenced by a later study of a different Mtb-specific protein modulating macrophage signalling (Rv1509, PMID 40129707), consistent with the immunomodulatory role.
