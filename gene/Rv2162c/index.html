Condition: Condition-blind by construction. These edges come from transcription-factor OVEREXPRESSION in a single laboratory condition, so a listed regulator is not a claim that it governs this gene in every circumstance. Read a regulator as 'capable of shifting this gene', and look for the condition before building a hypothesis on it.
