Two orthogonal outgroup signals. M. canettii (the immediate outgroup) gives a deep-divergence dN/dS (a low value confirms a constrained, real gene; shown as confident only at ≥8 substitutions, else flagged low-power). Genus-wide presence/absence (tblastn vs assembled non-MTBC genomes) places the gene on the ancient-core ↔ MTBC-specific axis: a gene absent even from the closest MTBAP relatives is a candidate MTBC-specific innovation (possible host-adaptation factor, to confirm by synteny). The phylostratum extends that axis outside the genus (tblastn vs 13 reference genomes spanning Mycobacteriaceae → Corynebacteriales → Actinomycetia → outside the phylum): it is the deepest clade in which a homolog is still detected, i.e. a proxy for gene age. Read it with the null model in mind: a shallow (young) stratum can also reflect homology-detection failure for short or fast-evolving ORFs, so it is a descriptive axis, not a proof of novelty.

Deep-dive (literature-grounded synthesis, hypothesis)

PE35 forms an obligate heterodimer with its operonic partner PPE68 (Rv3873, immediately downstream in the RD1/ESX-1 locus); the complex engages TLR2 on macrophages to bias cytokine output toward an anti-inflammatory, IL-10-dominated profile, and cell-associated PE35 also sits in a cytosolic complex with PPE68 and the EspG1 chaperone implicated in ESX-1-dependent secretion.

Evidence
  • Co-operonic with PPE68 in M. tuberculosis; the two gene products physically interact (co-immunoprecipitation, pull-down, DHFR reconstitution) (Tiwari et al. 2014).
  • Recombinant PE35+PPE68 stimulation of THP-1 macrophages gives a dose-dependent rise in IL-10 and MCP-1 and a reciprocal fall in IL-12, blocked by TLR2 receptor blockade and dependent on MAPK signalling (Tiwari et al. 2014).
  • Cell-associated PPE68 (in both M. marinum and M. tuberculosis) forms a cytosolic complex with its PE partner (PE35) and the EspG1 chaperone (Damen et al. 2022) -- see PPE68 fiche for the caveat that the causal secretion experiments were run in M. marinum.
  • PE35 shows significant clinical-strain sequence variability, more so in non-Beijing than Beijing-lineage isolates, at human T-cell epitope positions -- consistent with diversifying immune-evasion selection, though a direct virulence effect is not demonstrated (Jiang et al. 2016).
  • The entire RD1 locus carrying PE35-PPE68 is deleted in all M. bovis BCG vaccine strains -- already the textbook basis for RD1 as an attenuation/diagnostic marker, independent of this synthesis.

Caveats: This is genuine, causally-tested immunomodulatory evidence (receptor blockade, knockout/complementation), not a family-wide generalisation applied to an individual gene -- but it rests on a single research group's THP-1/recombinant-protein system for the cytokine data. This atlas's own data show PE35 non-essential in standard growth (CRISPRi VI=0.53, Tn-seq non-essential): its documented role is immune modulation and secretion, not growth fitness, so essentiality calls are not informative here.

Condition: Condition-blind by construction. These edges come from transcription-factor OVEREXPRESSION in a single laboratory condition, so a listed regulator is not a claim that it governs this gene in every circumstance. Measured counter-examples exist within this atlas (see Rv2516c, where the DosR regulon collapses under moxifloxacin while the gene itself rises).
