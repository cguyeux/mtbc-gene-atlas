Curated functionHigh affinity, slow turnover Mn(2+) transporting ATPase, which is required for virulence. Controls the Mn(2+) cytoplasmic quota and is involved in the uploading of Mn(2+) into secreted metalloproteins. Required for tolerance to Zn(2+) and oxidative stress. Plays a crucial role in the ability to resist zinc poisoning in human macrophages. Shows a preference for Mn(2+), but Zn(2+), Co(2+) and Cu(2+) can act as alternative substrates although at slower turnover rates.

Read it with the null model in mind: a shallow (young) stratum can also reflect homology-detection failure for short or fast-evolving ORFs, so it is a descriptive axis, not a proof of novelty.
