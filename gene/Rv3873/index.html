Curated reference (UniProt)

UniProt namePPE family immunomodulator PPE68
Curated functionPlays a major role in RD1-associated pathogenesis, and may contribute to the establishment and maintenance of M.tuberculosis infection. Together with PE35, stimulates the secretion of IL-10 and MCP-1 from human macrophages, via the interaction with human Toll-like receptor 2 (TLR2). Stimulates high levels of gamma interferon secretion in peripheral blood mononuclear cells isolated from tuberculosis patients, as well as from healthy tuberculin purified protein derivative-positive donors. Does not interfere with the secretion and immunogenicity of EsxA (ESAT-6) and EsxB (CFP-10).

Read the dataset count as abundance, not as replication. Deep-dive (literature-grounded synthesis, hypothesis)

PPE68 is the operonic and physical partner of PE35 (Rv3872) and the more mechanistically central of the pair: in Mycobacterium marinum it is required, via its EspG1-chaperoned cytosolic complex, for ESX-1-mediated secretion of the immunodominant substrates EsxA and EspE, and -- together with PE35 -- it engages TLR2 to skew macrophages toward an IL-10-biased, anti-inflammatory response.

Evidence
  • Co-operonic with PE35; physical interaction confirmed by co-IP/pull-down/DHFR reconstitution; TLR2-dependent IL-10/MCP-1 induction and IL-12 suppression on THP-1 macrophages, identical evidence base as PE35 (Tiwari et al. 2014).
  • In M. marinum, PPE68 is required for ESX-1 secretion of EsxA and EspE (a secretion role, distinct from and not contradicted by its non-essential standard-growth phenotype below): it is processed on the cell surface, sits in a cytosolic complex with its PE partner and the EspG1 chaperone, and blocking EspG1 binding blocks both PPE68 export and EsxA/EspE secretion -- while esxA itself is dispensable for PPE68 secretion, revealing a hierarchy. The final 10 residues of PPE68 are specifically required for EspE (not EsxA) secretion (Damen et al. 2022). **Caveat: demonstrated in M. marinum, not independently replicated in M. tuberculosis in this source** (cf. project guard-fou: 'better studied elsewhere' is not established function in Mtb).
  • This atlas's own Foldseek hits are structurally consistent: PPE68 (pLDDT 68.5) matches solved PE-PPE heterodimer crystal structures (PE8-PPE15, PE5-PPE4-EspG3) at TM>=0.85 and flagged significant (sig=True) -- a real structural anchor for the heterodimer model, not just a sequence-based inference.
  • STRING places PPE68's top partners as espG1, espE, PE35, esxB and espJ, all ESX-1 apparatus/substrates with high neighborhood+coexpression scores -- internally consistent with the secretion-complex literature above.
  • PPE68 shows strong in vivo phenotype signal in this atlas's own mouse-infection Tn-seq data (56 significant phenotypes, 55 in vivo, several fitness log2FC around -4 in mouse infection) -- a fitness cost on disruption during infection, consistent with (but not proof of) its proposed secretion role.

Caveats: The mechanistic secretion-hierarchy claim is from M. marinum; treat as a strong candidate mechanism for M. tuberculosis, not an established one there. CRISPRi/Tn-seq calls it non-essential for standard in vitro growth (VI=0.93, non-essential) despite the strong in vivo mouse-infection fitness signal above -- both can be true (a gene dispensable for growth but costly during infection); no essentiality claim is made without this reservation.
