gORF_113929 microprotein

Antisense to a gene MS-detected (high-confidence PSM) function unknown 58 aa

A non-canonical small protein (smORF) excluded from the H37Rv annotation. It was detected by proteogenomics with a high-confidence peptide-spectrum match (de Souza et al. 2024). It is served here as part of a separate microproteome track: it is not one of the 3906 canonical genes and does not count in the dark stock. Evidence of expression is not evidence of function: its molecular function is unknown. It is encoded antisense to PE_PGRS58 on the opposite strand.
