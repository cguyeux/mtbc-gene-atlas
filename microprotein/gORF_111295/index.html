gORF_111295 microprotein

Same-strand overlap (alternative frame) MS-detected (high-confidence PSM) function unknown 92 aa

A non-canonical small protein (smORF) excluded from the H37Rv annotation. It was detected by proteogenomics with a high-confidence peptide-spectrum match (de Souza et al. 2024). It is served here as part of a separate microproteome track: it is not one of the 3906 canonical genes and does not count in the dark stock. Evidence of expression is not evidence of function: its molecular function is unknown. It lies on the same strand within PPE8, i.e. an alternative reading frame of a known gene (dual coding) rather than a new locus.
