Literature characterised

Virulence-associated sRNA, actively studied 2019-2026 (12 papers): regulates transcriptional shifts for dormancy entry and macrophage survival (Salina et al. 2019); confers pathogenic properties on non-pathogenic M. smegmatis; detoxifies reactive oxygen species under oxidative stress; transcriptionally activated by DosR and PhoP; posttranscriptionally regulated by ribonuclease YbeY; binds the cold-shock protein CspB; and activates the ABC transporter CydC for rifampicin efflux (2024).

Read with care. The atlas track still carried only the 2011 discovery description ('putative small regulatory RNA', Arnvig et al.) — the gene has been under active functional study for seven years since.
